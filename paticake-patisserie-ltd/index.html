Our story

A family café,
baking since 2019.

Husband-and-wife team Pati and Ariel opened Paticake Patisserie in 2019, and it has since been recognised as the best coffee shop in Sunderland.

Come in for homemade cake, breakfast sandwiches, soup, dumplings, scones and real ice cream, with a proper coffee, chai latte or smoothie alongside.
